# About Me

I help teams automate data workflows, fix broken pipelines, and replace manual reporting with clean, reliable, automated systems.

With experience across Python, Azure, Databricks, SQL, and cloud-native data engineering, I’ve helped organizations turn messy data into streamlined pipelines, dashboards, and actionable insights.

## What I Solve
- Manual Excel workflows  
- Unreliable or broken ETL pipelines  
- Slow or inconsistent reporting  
- Data scattered across systems  
- Cloud cost overruns  
- Lack of visibility into business metrics  

## My Approach
Practical, fast, and focused on business outcomes.  
I design automation and pipelines that reduce workload, eliminate errors, and deliver reliable data for decision-making.
