<section class="problem-band"><div class="section-label">What I solve</div><div><h2>Technical debt is a business problem when it slows the work down.</h2><p>My focus is the space between “we know this needs fixing” and “we are ready to hire a full-time team.” That means getting oriented in an inherited codebase, tracing a data failure, adding tests before a risky change, and making the next handoff clearer.</p></div></section>
